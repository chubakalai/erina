Dietary Composition

This diet is heavily carb-centric, providing energy primarily through noodles, potatoes, and cornstarch. Protein is moderate and predominantly sourced from the high volume of noodles alongside the eggs. Fat content is kept low-to-moderate, driven mainly by the vegetable oil and egg yolks.

Micronutrient Advisory

While adequate for short-term calorie targets, the narrow food selection leaves significant gaps in micronutrients (Vitamins A, C, D, E, K, most B vitamins, and essential minerals like calcium). Long-term adherence without supplementation or diet diversification carries a high risk of nutritional deficiencies.

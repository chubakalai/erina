Fractionation of Erinacine A by Flash Chromatography1

Specification:

  • Chromatography mode: Normal-phase
  • Stationary phase: Silica gel, 15 µm spherical particles, 60 Å pore size, 25 g silica mass
  • Mobile phase: Ethyl acetate/acetonitrile, 2:8 (v/v)
  • Elution: Isocratic
  • Flow rate: 5 mL/min
  • Run time: 20 min
  • Injection volume: 3 mL of crude extract
  • Detection: UV at 340 nm
  • Fraction triggering: UV threshold of 10 mAU
  • Solvent removal: Evaporation under nitrogen
  • Number of runs: 85
1 Naumoska, K.; Gregori, A.; Albreht, A. Two-Dimensional Chromatographic Isolation of High Purity Erinacine A from Hericium erinaceus. J. Fungi 2025, 11(2), 150. https://doi.org/10.3390/jof11020150

Fractionation of Erinacine A by Flash Chromatography1

Parameter Specification
Chromatography mode Normal-phase (NP) flash chromatography
Purpose Fractionate the crude H. erinaceus extract and collect the fraction enriched in erinacine A
Instrument PuriFlash 5.250 system, Interchim, Montluçon, France
Software IntersoftX, version 10.19.347.11
Stationary phase PuriFlash silica cartridge
Silica High-capacity silica
Silica particle size 15 µm spherical particles
Pore size 60 Å
Silica cartridge loading 25 g
Mobile phase Ethyl acetate : acetonitrile = 2:8 (v/v)
Elution Isocratic
Flow rate 5 mL/min
Total run time 20 min
Injection volume 3 mL crude extract
UV monitoring 340 nm, with spectra acquired from 200–800 nm
ELSD monitoring ELSD at 40 °C
Fraction triggering UV detector threshold of 10 mAU
Fractions collected Four fractions, designated #1–#4
Erinacine-A-rich fraction Fraction #3
Scale-up/repetition Fractions from six consecutive analyses were pooled; solvent was removed under nitrogen and residue reconstituted in 2 mL of 70% aqueous ethanol
Total flash runs 85 fractionation runs
1 Naumoska, K.; Gregori, A.; Albreht, A. Two-Dimensional Chromatographic Isolation of High Purity Erinacine A from Hericium erinaceus. J. Fungi 2025, 11(2), 150. https://doi.org/10.3390/jof11020150
